About EcoNova

Recovering Resources, Creating Value & Building a Sustainable Future

EcoNova Waste Management Company is a Ghanaian waste management and recycling enterprise committed to promoting environmental sustainability through responsible waste collection, recycling and resource recovery.

We work with schools, universities, bookshops, offices, hospitals, commercial establishments, and manufacturing companies to ensure that recyclable waste is properly recovered and redirected into productive use.

Institutional Partners

Colleges, banks, printing presses & corporate offices.

Nationwide Network

Supervisors & collection agents across regions.

Our Core Divisions

We don't just collect waste. Our business model is structured into four robust operational divisions:

1. Waste Collection

Collecting recyclable materials efficiently from institutions and individuals.

2. Recycling & Recovery

Sorting, weighing, and preparing recovered materials for recycling.

3. Commercial Partnerships

Establishing mutually beneficial agreements with schools, companies and institutions.

4. Recycling Material Supply

Supplying verified recovered materials to tissue and manufacturing companies.

Our Vision

To become one of Ghana's leading waste management and recycling companies, recognised for innovation, environmental responsibility and excellence in transforming waste into valuable resources.

Our Mission

To provide reliable and sustainable waste collection and recycling solutions by recovering recyclable materials, building strategic partnerships and promoting responsible environmental practices.
